Our approach

We aim to make the website usable across common devices, screen sizes and input methods. The site uses semantic HTML, labelled form fields, visible focus states, keyboard-friendly controls and reduced-motion support.

Need help?

If you have difficulty accessing any content or using a feature, contact us and describe the problem. We will work to provide the information in a usable format where reasonably possible.
